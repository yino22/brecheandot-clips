Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

BRECHEANDOT Clips ("the App") is a desktop tool that runs on the creator's own computer and helps the creator send their own videos to their own TikTok account as drafts, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Information we access

When a creator connects their TikTok account, the App receives an access token and the creator's basic profile information (user ID and display name) through TikTok Login Kit. The App uses the token only to upload the videos the creator selects to that creator's own TikTok inbox.

Information we store

Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, in the App's local data folder. We do not operate servers that collect, store, sell or share creator data, videos or credentials.

Videos

Videos are transferred directly from the creator's computer to TikTok's API. They are not uploaded to, or kept on, any server of ours.

Revoking access

Creators can revoke the App's access at any time from their TikTok account settings, and can delete the locally stored tokens by removing the App's data folder from their computer.
