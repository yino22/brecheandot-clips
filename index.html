BRECHEANDOT Clips

BRECHEANDOT Clips is a desktop tool that helps creators prepare short vertical videos (with subtitles, titles, hooks and hashtags) and send them to their own social media accounts, including TikTok, for review and publishing.

How it works

The creator connects their own TikTok account, chooses a finished video on their computer, and the tool uploads it to their TikTok inbox as a draft. The creator then reviews and publishes it from the TikTok app. Nothing is posted publicly without the creator's own action in TikTok.
